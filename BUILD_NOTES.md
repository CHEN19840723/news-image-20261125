### Lead — Pakistan's first astronaut reaches orbit (Wednesday)
- China's Shenzhou-24 launched at 08:18 CST (00:18 UTC) on Wednesday
  from Jiuquan, carrying Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi and Pakistani payload
  specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, 40, to the Tiangong space station.
  Ali is the first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign astronaut
  aboard Tiangong; he will spend 5–10 days running experiments. The
  first joint Chinese–foreign crewed flight in China's space programme.
